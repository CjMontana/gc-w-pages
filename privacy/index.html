Witly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Product of Glimpse Concepts

Witly is a user-initiated reply co-pilot. You choose a screenshot or share conversation text; Witly suggests reply options you can copy and send. This policy explains what we process and why.

Who we are

Witly is operated by Glimpse Concepts (the “Company”, “we”, “us”). Contact: privacy@glimpseconcepts.com.

What Witly does

You open Witly (or its floating button), pick or capture a screenshot, and optionally add context about yourself or the person you are messaging. We generate reply suggestions for you to review. We do not monitor other apps in the background for chat content.

Data we process

  • Screenshots and shared images or text you explicitly submit for analysis.
  • Optional context you add (goals, notes, style preferences, profile details).
  • Account data if you sign in (for example email via our auth provider).
  • Usage and diagnostics needed to run the service (limits, errors, device basics).

AI processing

When you start an analysis, screenshot bytes and related text leave your device and are sent to our hosted analysis gateway, which calls third-party AI providers (currently via OpenRouter) to draft replies. Do not submit content you are not comfortable processing with those providers.

Retention

  • We do not intentionally keep raw screenshots after processing. Short-lived local cache files may exist until cleared.
  • Suggestion rounds may be stored briefly on device (for example up to 24 hours).
  • Contacts, styles, and context documents you save remain until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Conversation snippets and chosen replies may be kept to personalize history (default window described in-app / product docs).

What we do not do

  • We do not continuously read other apps’ screens for content.
  • We do not sell your personal conversation content.
  • We do not use your screenshots to train our own public models.

Permissions

Depending on platform, Witly may request access such as photos or files (to pick screenshots), microphone (optional voice context), overlay / display over other apps (floating button on Android), and notifications related to the overlay service. You can revoke permissions in system settings.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in Settings. That removes synced cloud data associated with your account and clears local app data on the device where you confirm deletion, subject to normal backup and legal retention limits.

Children

Witly is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
