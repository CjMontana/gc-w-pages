Witly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Product of Glimpse Concepts

Witly is a user-initiated reply co-pilot. You choose a screenshot or share conversation text; Witly suggests reply options you can copy and send. This policy explains what we process and why.

Who we are

Witly is operated by Glimpse Concepts (the “Company”, “we”, “us”). Contact: privacy@glimpseconcepts.com.

What Witly does

You open Witly (or its floating button), pick or capture a screenshot, and optionally add context about yourself or the person you are messaging. We generate reply suggestions for you to review. We do not monitor other apps in the background for chat content. If you allow optional Photos access, Witly may show a tip for a recent Screenshots-album image when you open the app; analysis still starts only after you choose that tip and confirm Analyze.

Data we process

  • Screenshots and shared images or text you explicitly submit for analysis.
  • Optional context you add (goals, notes, style preferences, profile details).
  • Account data if you sign in (for example email via our auth provider).
  • Crash diagnostics collected so we can keep Witly stable (crash reports and light device and app info). We do not include chat text or screenshots in those reports. Processed using Google Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Optional product analytics off unless you opt in. When enabled, we may collect product usage events (not chat text or screenshots). Processed using Google Firebase Analytics when enabled.
  • Product feedback you submit in Settings (message, category, your account email, and light app/device diagnostics when you leave that option on).

AI processing

When you start an analysis, screenshot bytes and related text leave your device and are sent to our analysis service, which uses third-party AI providers to draft replies. Do not submit content you are not comfortable sharing for that processing.

Retention

  • We do not intentionally keep raw screenshots after processing. Short-lived local cache files may exist until cleared.
  • Suggestion rounds may be stored briefly on device (for example up to 24 hours).
  • Contacts, styles, and context documents you save remain until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Product feedback you submit is kept so we can improve the product, until you delete your account or we remove handled notes.
  • Conversation snippets and chosen replies may be kept to personalize history (default window described in-app / product docs).

What we do not do

  • We do not continuously read other apps’ screens for content.
  • We do not sell your personal conversation content.
  • We do not use your screenshots to train our own public models.

Permissions

Depending on platform, Witly may request access such as photos or media (to pick screenshots, and optionally to suggest a recent Screenshots-album image when you open the app), microphone (optional voice context), overlay / display over other apps (floating button on Android), and notifications related to the overlay service. You can revoke permissions in system settings.

Account deletion

Witly (operated by Glimpse Concepts) lets you delete your account and associated data. You do not need to keep the app installed to request deletion.

Delete in the Witly app (recommended)

  1. Open Witly and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Witly cloud account and synced data, then clears Witly data on the device where you confirm deletion (including short-lived screenshot cache). It cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Witly installed, email privacy@glimpseconcepts.com with subject line Witly account deletion request. Include the email address on your Witly account (Google/Apple/email sign-in). We will verify ownership and delete the account and associated cloud data. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days (sooner when practical).

What we delete

  • Your Witly authentication account (sign-in identity tied to Witly).
  • Synced cloud data for that account (for example contacts, chat styles, context documents, and history synced to our servers).
  • On the device where you use in-app delete: local Witly database rows for your account, auth session, and ephemeral screenshot cache.

What may remain

  • Data we must keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting obligations, for as long as required.
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics that no longer identify you.
  • Copies in device/OS backups outside Witly’s control (you can clear those from the device/backup settings).
  • Content already processed by third-party AI providers for a past analysis, subject to those providers’ retention terms (we do not intentionally keep raw screenshots after processing).

Children

Witly is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).
